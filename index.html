THE STORY

Small arcade.
Real character.

REDCORE started with one simple idea: a game site should feel like a place, not a pile of cards. Every title stays one click away, while the surrounding experience remains calm, fast and unmistakably REDCORE.

There is no feed to chase. Choose a game, make a run, set a score, and return when the mood is right.

PRIVACY POLICY

Privacy, in plain language.

REDCORE stores only the account and play activity needed to provide sign-in and player-profile features. Game activity may be saved through the configured Firebase services when you choose to sign in. We do not sell personal information. Third-party games and services may have their own policies; review them before use.

To ask about your data, contact the REDCORE developer through the linked Instagram profile.
